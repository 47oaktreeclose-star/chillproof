ChillProof

Privacy Policy · Effective 25 October 2026

Summary

ChillProof is a fridge and freezer temperature logging app for food businesses. Your business details and temperature records are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not sell or share your information.

Information you enter

  • Business name, email address, telephone number and (optionally) company registration number.
  • Names of your fridges and freezers, temperature readings, corrective actions, and the name or initials of the person who took the check.

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is used to show your records in the app and to create temperature logs.

Emailing your logs

When you choose to email a log, ChillProof opens your own email app with the log and a PDF attached, addressed to your registered email. The email is sent by your email provider, not by us. We never receive a copy.

Payments

Subscriptions and one-off purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your card details. Google's privacy policy applies to payment information: policies.google.com/privacy. The app checks with Google Play which purchases your Google account owns so it can unlock features.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No reminder data is sent to us.

Analytics and advertising

ChillProof contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete all of your details and records at any time in Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes this data. Because we do not hold your data on servers, there is nothing further for us to delete. Cancelling your subscription is done in Google Play and does not delete your records.

Children

ChillProof is a business tool and is not intended for children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you have rights over your personal data. As your data is held on your own device, you can view, change or delete it directly in the app. If you have any questions, contact us.
